## Forks and redistributions

The AGPL lets you fork and redistribute the code. If you distribute a modified version,
you must **remove the Kalaido Marks** (name in the UI, logos, icons, splash and store
listings) and give it a name that is not confusingly similar. You may state accurately
that it is "based on Kalaido".

Unmodified builds of official releases may keep the marks.
