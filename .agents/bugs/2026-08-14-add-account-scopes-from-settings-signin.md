## Description
When signing in from the settings menu, there is currently no way to add the scopes associated with the account into the local scope selection dropdown.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open settings.
2. Sign into an account.
3. Look for an option or flow to add/import the account's scopes to the local scope selection dropdown.

## Expected Behavior
There should be a way to add the scopes associated with the signed-in account to the local dropdown.

## Observed Behavior
There is no option or mechanism to add the account's scopes to the local dropdown after signing in from settings.
